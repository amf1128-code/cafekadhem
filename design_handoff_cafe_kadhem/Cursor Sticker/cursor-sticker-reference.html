World Cup Watch Party

Football, free knafeh croissants at halftime, and yelling at the screen. Move your cursor around. Click. The sticker should follow and change phrase.

صحتين

Visual spec

  • Pill shape · border-radius: 999px
  • Background: --sun (#FF8E2C)
  • 2px solid ink border + 2px 2px 0 ink shadow (hard offset)
  • Padding: 8px 14px
  • Font: Rakkas (Arabic display), 22px, line-height 1, direction rtl
  • Rotation: -10deg to -14deg (subtle tilt, not cute-cartoonish)

Behavior

  • Position: absolute on a relatively-positioned stage
  • Offset from cursor: +20px x, -30px y
  • Use requestAnimationFrame to throttle mousemove → DOM transform writes (do NOT call setState every move; that nukes performance)
  • pointer-events: none so it never blocks clicks
  • Fade in on first move, fade out on mouseleave (200ms)
  • Click anywhere in the stage cycles to next phrase
  • mix-blend-mode: multiply optional — looks nice on light bg, can omit

Phrases (cycle in order)

صحتين بالهنا اتفضل يلا طازة حبيبي
  • صحتين (sahteen) — "to your health" (default)
  • بالهنا (bil hana) — "with pleasure"
  • اتفضل (itfaddal) — "go ahead / help yourself"
  • يلا (yalla) — "let's go"
  • طازة (taza) — "fresh"
  • حبيبي (habibi) — "my dear"

Where it lives

  • Mounts on the landing page root (the .cinema-root scroll container)
  • Disabled on touch devices ((pointer: coarse) media query — no hover, no value)
  • Disabled when modals are open (don't fight the RSVP modal)
  • Disabled inside form fields and the audio button (otherwise it covers UI)
